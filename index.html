About us

FROM FIRST GLIDE
TO FEARLESS RIDES.

The Skating Club at IIT Kanpur is where passion meets progress. Whether you're stepping onto skates for the very first time or aiming to master advanced techniques, every journey begins with expert coaching and a supportive team. Through structured training, daily practice sessions, exciting workshops, and thrilling competitions, we transform beginners into confident skaters and teammates into lifelong friends. Here, every fall is a lesson, every lap is progress, and every achievement is celebrated together.

FAQs

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS.

Everything you need to know before stepping onto the rink.

Do I need prior skating experience?

No. Beginners are always welcome! Our coaches and coordinators will guide you from the basics.

Are skates and safety gear provided?

Yes. The club provides skates along with helmets and protective gear during practice sessions.

Who can join the Skating Club?

All IIT Kanpur students—including UG, PG, and PhD students—are welcome to join.

When are practice sessions conducted?

Regular practice sessions are held Monday to Saturday during the evening at the skating rink.

What should I bring?

Wear comfortable sports clothing and socks. The club will provide the skating equipment.
